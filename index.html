How to Play Click the Circle

Click the red circle as quickly as possible whenever it appears. Each successful click increases your score, while the circle becomes smaller and harder to hit.

  1. Press Start Game to begin.
  2. Click or tap the red circle.
  3. Keep clicking as quickly as you can.
  4. The circle gets smaller as your score increases.
  5. Try to get the highest score possible in 30 seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Click the Circle free?

Yes. Click the Circle is a free browser game that you can play without installing an app.

How long does a game last?

Each game lasts 30 seconds.

Does the game save my high score?

Yes. Your best score is saved in your browser so you can try to beat it later.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game supports both mouse clicks and touch input.
