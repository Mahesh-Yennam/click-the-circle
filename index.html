FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Click the Circle completely free?

Yes! Click the Circle is 100% free with no downloads, sign-ups, or microtransactions required. Play directly in your browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

How does the 30-second timer work?

Once you hit Start, you have exactly 30 seconds to click as many circles as you can. When the timer hits zero, the game ends and your score is ranked.

Does the game save my High Score?

Yes. Your all-time personal best is automatically saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back anytime and beat your record.

Can I play on smartphones or tablets?

Absolutely. The game features zero-latency touch support tailored specifically for iOS and Android touchscreens without double-tap delay.
